Python service berhasil di-deploy via Helm!

Akses aplikasi:
  export NODE_IP=$(kubectl get nodes -o jsonpath="{.items[0].status.addresses[0].address}")
  curl http://$NODE_IP:{{ .Values.service.nodePort }}

Cek status:
  helm status {{ .Release.Name }}
  kubectl get pods -l app.kubernetes.io/name=python-service-chart
